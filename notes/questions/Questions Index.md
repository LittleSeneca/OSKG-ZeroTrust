# Questions Index

Open research questions that the knowledge graph is tracking. These are the questions that drive claims extraction and synthesis — what we need the graph to answer.

## Architectural Questions

- What is the canonical definition of Zero Trust, and where do definitions diverge?
- Is Zero Trust a paradigm shift or a rebranding of existing security principles?
- Where does Zero Trust end and something else (SASE, SSE, identity management) begin?
- Can Zero Trust be implemented without a full network architecture redesign?
- What is the relationship between Zero Trust and defense in depth — complementary or competing?

## Implementation Questions

- What is the minimum viable Zero Trust architecture?
- In what order should organizations implement Zero Trust capabilities?
- Does ZTNA actually replace VPNs, or is that vendor positioning?
- How does Zero Trust work in OT/ICS environments where devices can't run agents?
- What is the role of microsegmentation in a practical Zero Trust implementation?

## Evidence Questions

- What empirical evidence exists that Zero Trust reduces breach impact or frequency?
- How many organizations have achieved CISA Maturity Model Level 3 (Advanced)?
- What is the failure rate of Zero Trust initiatives, and what causes failures?

## Framework Questions

- Do NIST 800-207, CISA Maturity Model, and DoD ZT Reference Architecture define compatible architectures?
- Is the CSA CCZT body of knowledge consistent with NIST, or does it introduce divergence?
- How does Zero Trust relate to compliance — does it help, hinder, or operate orthogonally?

## Meta Questions

- How much of the Zero Trust literature is vendor-neutral vs. vendor-driven?
- Which claims in the Zero Trust space are genuinely evidence-based vs. asserted from authority?
- Where is the Zero Trust consensus — and where is it just a consensus of the same small group of authors?
