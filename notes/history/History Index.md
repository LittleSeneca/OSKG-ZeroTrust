# History Index

The evolution of Zero Trust thinking from its origins to the present. Timeline, key publications, and the intellectual lineage of the ideas.

## Timeline

| Year | Event | Significance |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 2004 | Jericho Forum founded | Predecessor concept: "de-perimeterisation" |
| 2010 | Kindervag publishes "Build Security Into Your Network's DNA" (Forrester) | **Zero Trust is named and defined.** |
| 2012 | Kindervag publishes "No More Chewy Centers" | Expands ZT beyond the network layer |
| 2014 | Google publishes BeyondCorp | First major tech company to operationalize ZT principles (not under the ZT name) |
| 2017 | Gilman & Barth publish "Zero Trust Networks" (O'Reilly) | First comprehensive book on ZT architecture |
| 2018 | Chase Cunningham defines ZTX framework (Forrester) | Extends ZT across the full security ecosystem |
| 2019 | Gartner coins SASE; ZTNA emerges as a category | Zero Trust principles begin to influence adjacent security markets |
| 2020 | NIST publishes SP 800-207 | ZT receives formal government standardization |
| 2021 | CISA publishes ZT Maturity Model; Biden Executive Order 14028 | ZT becomes federal mandate; maturity measurement framework established |
| 2022 | DoD publishes ZT Reference Architecture v2.0; Finney publishes "Project Zero Trust" | Military operationalization; organizational narrative |
| 2023 | CSA releases CCZT certification | First vendor-neutral ZT body of knowledge and certification |
| 2024 | NIST SP 800-207A; Green-Ortiz et al. publish "Zero Trust Architecture" | Cloud-native extension; comprehensive textbook |
| 2025 | Halley et al. publish "Zero Trust in Resilient Cloud" | Cloud-native deployment patterns; quantum and OT ZT |

## Key Debates

- **ZTA vs. ZTX vs. SASE:** What is the scope of Zero Trust, and where does it intersect with adjacent frameworks?
- **Product vs. Strategy:** Is Zero Trust something you buy or something you architect? The tension between vendor positioning and architectural principle.
- **Greenfield vs. Brownfield:** Can Zero Trust be retrofitted, or does it require a clean-slate approach?
- **Network-centric vs. Identity-centric:** Where does the primary enforcement happen?
