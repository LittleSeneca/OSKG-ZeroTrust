## Brief Format

Each evidence brief follows the OSKG standard:

1. **Question** — What is being asked, precisely defined
2. **Claims Inventory** — Every claim relevant to the question, with source and edge context
3. **Convergence Map** — Where claims agree
4. **Fault Lines** — Where claims genuinely conflict
5. **Weight Assessment** — What the preponderance of evidence supports
6. **Unknowns** — What remains genuinely unanswered
