# bsi-zt.9: BSI plans market surveillance, IT-Grundschutz integration, and sector-specific guidance

**Source:** [[BSI — Zero Trust Position Paper]] — BSI, *Zero Trust Position Paper*, 2023

## The Claim

"Das BSI plant eine Marktsichtung zur Analyse von Zero Trust-Funktionen in Produkten durchzuführen. Zusätzlich wird das BSI weiterhin die IT-Grundschutz-Anforderungen – auch unter Berücksichtigung von Zero Trust-Prinzipien - weiterentwickeln."

Translation: The BSI plans a market survey to analyze ZT functionality in products and will continue developing IT-Grundschutz (IT Baseline Protection) requirements incorporating ZT principles. Organizations can already consider ZT in their risk analyses now, independently of further concretization.

## Confidence

**Rating:** HIGH
**Rationale:** HIGH for what is stated. LOW for what has actually occurred — as of this note's creation (July 2026), I have not independently verified whether the market survey or IT-Grundschutz ZT integration has been published.

## Stakes

IT-Grundschutz is Germany's mandatory baseline security framework for federal IT. If ZT principles are integrated into IT-Grundschutz, they become de facto mandatory for German federal agencies — making this the most consequential national ZT adoption mechanism in Europe.
