# gc-soc-data-iot.2: SOC integration should be pursued early in the ZT journey, not deferred to a later phase — having the SOC team on board accelerates adoption across the enterprise.

**Source:** [[Garbis and Chapman — SOC Data IoT]] — Jason Garbis & Jerry Chapman, *Zero Trust Security: An Enterprise Guide*, 2021

## The Claim

The authors state that SOC integration "should be part of your ZT journey early" because SOAR codifies tribal knowledge into repeatable playbooks and its reach across enterprise security infrastructure makes it "a natural partner for a Zero Trust platform."

## Evidence

The book describes SOAR as consuming events/alerts from SIEM and providing automated or semi-automated response workflows. Beyond automation, SOAR captures the "tribal knowledge that otherwise lives only in senior analysts' heads into repeatable, reliable playbooks." Its integration of people, process, and technology creates natural alignment with ZT's cross-domain scope.
