# Synthesis Index

Capstone synthesis documents that emerge from the structured analysis of the knowledge graph.

## Synthesis Phases

The synthesis follows the same 4-phase structure as OSKG-YahWeh:

1. **Hinge Inventory** — Which claims are load-bearing? If they fall, what collapses?
2. **Cascade Trees** — Dependency chains for critical claims. What does each hinge claim depend on?
3. **Counter-Position Stress Tests** — What would the strongest opponent of each major position argue? What survives?
4. **Convergence and Unknowns** — Where do sources genuinely converge? What remains genuinely unsettled?

## Planned Capstone

**"Zero Trust: What the Evidence Shows"** — The culminating synthesis document. An evidence-forward report on what the knowledge graph reveals about Zero Trust architecture: what is settled, what is contested, what is load-bearing, and what claims don't survive scrutiny.

**No synthesis documents yet.** Synthesis begins after Phase 2 (Claims Extraction) produces a populated knowledge graph. Expected timeline: dependent on source acquisition and reading velocity.
